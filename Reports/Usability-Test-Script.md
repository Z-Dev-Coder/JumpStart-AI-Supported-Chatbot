# JumpStart AI Support — Exploratory Usability Test Script

Per Proposal §7.8: 3 genuine participants, intentionally small/non-representative, for surfacing obvious interface/navigation/handover problems before final evaluation. Conducted in-person or via screen-share, with the researcher observing (not self-administered like the questionnaire).

Estimated session length: 10-15 minutes per participant.

---

## Before the session

- Explain: "This is an early-stage AI customer support prototype for a fictional retailer, JumpStart. I'd like you to try completing a few realistic tasks while I observe. There are no wrong answers — if something is confusing, that's useful information, not a mistake on your part."
- Get verbal consent to observe/take notes. No recording of identifying information — anonymous per Proposal.
- Open the chat interface fresh (new/incognito session) before they start.

## Tasks (ask participant to think aloud while completing each)

**Task 1 — General question**
"Imagine you're wondering whether you can return a pair of headphones. Find out using the chat."
- Observe: do they find the chat entry point easily? Do they read the response, or does it look unclear?

**Task 2 — A question needing follow-up**
"Ask the chat if you can return 'an item' — just that, nothing more specific — and see what happens."
- Observe: does the clarifying question make sense to them? Do they understand *why* it's being asked?

**Task 3 — Something the bot can't help with**
"Ask the chat something unrelated to shopping, like what the weather is today."
- Observe: is the decline/redirect message clear? Do they understand why it can't help?

**Task 4 — Requesting a human**
"Ask to speak to a real person."
- Observe: is it obvious what happens next (queue message, handover indicator)? Do they feel like they know what to expect?

**Task 5 — Free exploration**
"Spend a minute asking anything else you're curious about."
- Observe: what do they naturally try? Anything that breaks or confuses them unprompted?

## After the session (short debrief questions, verbal)

1. "On a scale of 1-5, how easy was that overall?"
2. "Was there any point where you weren't sure what to do next?"
3. "Did anything feel confusing, slow, or untrustworthy?"
4. "Any single thing you'd change first?"

## Analysis approach (per Proposal §7.10)

Report as **exploratory evidence only** — 3 participants is not a claim of representativeness. Group observations into the same thematic categories as the questionnaire (trust, answer clarity, privacy, handover visibility, interface difficulty) so both data sources can be discussed side by side in the Findings report, even though they're different methods (behavioural observation vs. self-report).
